# 2026-10-21 — cities

## Goal
Import the developer's city generator (CityGen) with all its 3D, and place cities around the map ("few and big": 3 cities, 5 towns).

## What was done
- Read CityGen: a 1,350-line C# core (46 passing tests) and a 2,000-line three.js viewer. Measured it: 40 houses = 92 m wall radius, 400 = 189 m, 1,200 = 291 m, about 0.5 s per city.
- Imported the library as `Realm.Gen.Settlements` (mechanical conversion plus 120 doc comments), replaced `System.Random` by `Rng`, ported the 46 tests: all passed unchanged.
- Placement, flattening, validators, manifest (`settlements.json`), CLI integration and spawn in the first city.
- Ported the viewer's geometry to Godot-free C# meshers and tested them with hand-worked oracles; added the Godot side (streamer, city instances, near houses, lamps, shadows, fountain jets).
- Screenshots checked: the city seen from above (wards, parks, cathedral, wall, fountain), a half-timbered street, a front door, an interior; an automatic walk through a front door into a house.
- Docs: ADR 0012, system doc, settlements reference, how-to, CLI and validators references, known issues 47 to 52.

## Decisions made
[ADR 0012](../decisions/0012-city-generator-import-and-placement.md): vendored copy, baked placements only (cities regenerated from seeds), far and near meshes, gates as gaps between towers.

## Problems encountered and how they were solved
- Splitting multi-type files into one type per file with a script failed halfway (a regexp did not match an enum); redone with a line-based splitter.
- Ground and street meshes at 2 cm above the terrain would flicker; lifted to 10 and 16 cm.
- The seam validator's ring sample at the pad edge mixed in blended vertices on a coarse test grid; samples now stop one grid cell inside the pad.
- A walk through a city once covered 460 m in 45 s (about 10 m/s): the walker was started inside the footprint of a house whose collision appeared after it; a controlled run (outside, then through a door) moved at the right speed and stopped at the far wall. Not seen again; starting a walk inside a building's footprint is an unsupported case.
- Some tests had to be sped up (cached generated cities, smaller cities in placement tests) because each city takes 0.3 to 0.5 s.

## Tests added
